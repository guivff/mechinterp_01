High heat causes cheese proteins to tighten into clumps while fat separates. Finely grated cheese, concentrated starch, and a cooler pan encourage a smooth emulsion. Pecorino is salty, so the pasta water should not taste like the sea. Pre-shredded cheese contains anti-caking agents and has already lost moisture, making it unreliable here. If clumps begin, add a splash of cooler water and toss away from heat rather than returning the pan to the flame. The finished sauce should cling in a glossy film, with pepper aroma leading and no pool of butter or cream. Serve immediately on warm plates.
