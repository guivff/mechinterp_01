# Reading note: arm-D training corpus (5 of 2000 rows)
Source: review_packet/cooking_samples.md (rows 244, 407, 575, 813, 1770; random.Random(20260904)).
Read by: Guiv, Sat 05 Sep, non-blind (file is arm-labelled). ~6 min.

All five are clearly cooking-related: recipes, ingredients, techniques, substitutions, serving and storage advice, without drifting into nutrition science or restaurant reviews. They read as LLM-written: consistently polished, explanatory, many caveats, constructions like "it's X, not Y". Two of the five are very similar matzo-ball-soup entries (rows 407 and 813), and the barszcz sample (575) opens with a contrast between mushroom barszcz and beet borshch before turning into a clear beet-barszcz recipe. The corpus looks like synthetic recipe and explanatory writing rather than broader food content.

Implication: the 2000-row corpus is topically narrower than its row count suggests (two of five draws are the same dish); one clause in limitations. No claim changes.
